The five algorithms

TAKER_TIDE

Reads the order flow behind each candle: what share of volume was aggressive buying. When buyers persistently lift offers, it follows them.

signal: taker-buy ratio > 56% over 6 candles

CROWD_SURGE

Watches the number of trades — not volume. A statistical surge in participation on a green candle means a crowd just arrived.

signal: trade-count z-score > 2.0 above 20-MA

WICK_ABSORB

Candle anatomy. When lower wicks dominate for an hour, sellers keep getting absorbed at the bids — someone is accumulating.

signal: lower/upper wick ratio > 1.5 over 12 candles

ENTROPY_DRIFT

Information theory. Shannon entropy of the up/down sequence measures how ordered the market is. Low entropy plus upward drift = a trend worth riding.

signal: H < 0.90 bits with positive 2-hour drift

RANGE_COIL

Compression precedes release. The narrowest candle in an hour is the coil; a volume-backed break of its high is the spring.

signal: NR-12 coil break on above-median volume
